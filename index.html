Could a desert on Earth help us prepare for Mars? Could an Arctic crater teach us about the Moon? Earth analogs are places whose landscapes and conditions resemble those found beyond our planet. Studying them helps scientists and engineers prepare equipment, test ideas, and plan safer future missions.
